# PeduC local evaluation

## Purpose

Give an innovation cell, educator, or prospective sponsor a working application they can test before funding hosting, AI services, and content expansion. The requested target window is January–February 2027.

## What to observe

- Can a tester find a suitable lesson and understand the interest connection?
- Does manipulating the model help them explain the relationship, including its limitations?
- Can they move from the example to formal notation and an unfamiliar problem?
- Which checks required hints, retries, or the text coach?
- Can they distinguish sequence completion from evidence of independent understanding?
- Does the guide provide a useful authored explanation or clearly acknowledge its limited coverage?
- Can the tester return to saved progress and export their own evaluation?

The local pilot page records clarity and interest-relevance ratings, written observations, and the tester's actual attempt/coaching history. It does not create scores for participants who have not used it.

## What these records do not prove

Usability feedback and success on a few draft questions cannot establish learning efficiency, exam readiness, or causal benefit from interest-based instruction. Question exposure, hints, prior knowledge, and repeated attempts all affect apparent success.

An educational evaluation should use educator-reviewed, comparable baseline and follow-up assessments, unseen transfer tasks, and delayed recall, ideally with an appropriate comparison condition. Record sample size, recruitment, prior knowledge, assistance, missing data, and uncertainty. Do not import numerical efficacy claims from earlier prototype documents.

## Cost boundary

The current application has no per-message API fee and does not require a domain, email service, or purchased hosting for use on a tester's own computer. It still requires a suitable computer, Node.js, and internet for the first dependency installation. Human content creation, expert review, and study work are real costs even when the prototype has no cloud bill.

After the local evaluation, estimate funding separately for curriculum authoring, reviewed question banks, pedagogy evaluation, production identity/database/hosting, optional AI inference, and support. A credible budget requires expected usage and service choices; no unsupported total is presented here.

## Data boundaries

Each local profile has its own evidence and export. Reports contain only the exporting account's answers, conversation, and feedback. They are not automatically shared. Trial profiles are session-bound and have no email recovery. Source packages exclude learner databases, sessions, and environment secrets.

## Known release gaps

Four development lessons are available across the four subjects. Full CBSE/examination coverage, formal expert approval, a general AI tutor, multi-user institution roles, production-scale storage, recovery, and a controlled educational study remain future work. The logo and specific external design reference pages could not be fetched from the current environment.
